# The AI Employee Canvas 🧑‍🏭

> **Print this. Fill it by hand before you touch the code.**
> If you can answer these 8 questions in plain words, you can build the robot.
> This canvas IS your `my-agent.md` — you're just filling the same blanks the code reads.

Think of it like a job description for a new hire. You wouldn't hire a person without
telling them what to do, what to watch, and when to check with you first. Same here.

---

## Before you start

Write the two things that make it yours:

**Robot's name:** ________________________________   (e.g. "Chaser", "Nadia", "Money Mouse")

**Who's the boss it reports to:** ________________________________   (the approver — YOU, or a teammate's Telegram id)

---

## The 8 questions ✍️

Answer every one in one plain sentence. No jargon. If you can't say it simply, you don't
understand the job yet — and neither will the robot.

## The magic sentence (say it out loud when you're done) 🎤

Fill the blanks from your answers above — this is question 1 + 3 + 4 in one breath:

> **"When ______________ happens, my robot ______________ —
> but it asks ME before ______________."**

If that sentence is true and safe, your canvas is done. Now open
`agents/_template/README.md` and turn the 4 knobs — it's the same 8 answers, just in the file.

---

### Quick check before you build ☑️
- [ ] Every RED-list item is something the robot *cannot* do, not just *shouldn't*.
- [ ] Nothing customer-facing is on the GREEN list (drafts only — you send).
- [ ] "Move money" and "delete" appear nowhere except RED.
- [ ] Your magic sentence has an "asks ME before ___" — if it doesn't, it's not safe yet.
