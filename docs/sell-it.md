# Sell It — packaging CashFlowOS as a RM5k–50k offer

> You just built a Money Robot. Other business owners will pay you to build theirs. This is the one-pager for turning "I made a thing" into "I sell this for RM5k–50k." Keep it simple: you're not selling software — you're selling **a business that runs itself while the owner sleeps**.

---

## What you're actually selling (say it in one line)

**"I install an AI operations layer on your business — it files your money, chases what you're owed, watches your leads, and asks you before it spends a cent. You approve from your phone. That's it."**

You are NOT selling: "an app," "automation," "AI." Owners don't buy those. You're selling the **outcome**: hours back, nothing falling through the cracks, and never wondering where the cash is.

---

| | **Starter — RM5k** | **Operator — RM15k–25k** ⭐ | **Enterprise — RM50k** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Solo / small biz, RM300k–1M/yr | Real SME, RM1–5M/yr, has staff | Multi-branch / team, RM5M+ |

> **Pricing rule:** never sell the bottom. The RM5k tier exists to make RM20k look reasonable. Most owners land in the middle — that's where you want them. If they only want Starter, they're probably not your buyer yet.

---

## Why they pay (the value math — do this on the call)

Owners don't buy on price, they buy on **return**. Make it their arithmetic, out loud:

- *"How many hours a week do you or your staff spend chasing receipts, invoices, and follow-ups?"* → say it's 10.
- *"What's an hour of that person's time worth?"* → say RM50. That's **RM500/week = RM26k/year** you're handing back.
- *"How many invoices slip through unpaid because nobody chased?"* → one RM3k invoice recovered pays for half the build.

**So a RM20k install that returns RM26k/year in time alone + recovered cash = paid back in under a year, then free forever.** That's not an expense, that's a hire that costs once.

## The offer stack (make RM20k feel like a steal)

Stack value, then name the price last:

1. **The install** — CashFlowOS built + their real data imported (the thing).
2. **The agents** — 3–4 of their actual painful workflows, automated with the ask-first safety.
3. **The Vault** — snap any receipt/invoice → filed automatically. (The "wow" in every demo.)
4. **Team access + custom tabs** — built for how THEIR business works, not generic.
5. **Training** — 2 sessions so they and their staff actually use it.
6. **The safety guarantee** — nothing spends money or messages a customer without their YES. (De-risks the "AI will do something dumb" fear.)
7. **30-day settle-in** — you're on WhatsApp for the first month.

*"All of that — normally I'd quote RM30k+ for a build this custom. For you, RM20k, done in 3 weeks."*

---

## The guarantee (kills the risk objection)

Pick ONE and mean it:

- **"Runs it or refund it"** — *"If it's not filing your money and giving you your daily brief within 30 days of go-live, I refund you and you keep everything built."*
- **"One workflow, free trial"** — for cold prospects: *"Let me automate ONE painful thing free. If it saves you the time I say it will, we build the rest."* (Lands the whale later.)

A guarantee costs you almost nothing (you built it once, the safety rails mean it won't misfire) and removes the only real objection.

## The pitch (5 lines, owner-to-owner, no jargon)

> **Hook:** "You're still filing receipts by hand and chasing invoices yourself, right?"
> **Promise:** "I install an AI ops layer that does that for you — you just approve from your phone."
> **Proof:** "I run my own business on it. Snap a receipt, it's filed. Someone owes me, it drafts the chase. Takes me 5 minutes a day."
> **Price:** "Full install, your data, your workflows — RM20k, live in 3 weeks."
> **CTA:** "Want me to show you your version on a 20-minute call?"

One CTA. One call. No menu of options.

## Where to find the RM5k–50k buyers

- **Your own network first** — every SME owner you know is drowning in admin. Start there.
- **The demo IS the ad** — post your own Money Robot filing a receipt / giving your daily brief. "Built this for my business. Building them for 3 others this month. DM 'ROBOT'."
- **One niche at a time** — F&B, clinics, agencies, property. Same build, swap the tabs. Referrals compound inside a niche.
- **Land one, screenshot the result** — a real owner's "recovered RM8k in unpaid invoices in month one" is worth more than any pitch.

---

## The one thing to remember

You're not competing on "AI." You're the person who **installs calm** into a chaotic business — money filed, invoices chased, nothing forgotten, owner in control. Price that at what a year of an admin hire costs, guarantee it, and sell one at a time. RM20k × 2 a month = a RM480k/year business off a repo you already built once.
